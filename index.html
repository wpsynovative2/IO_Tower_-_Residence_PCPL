Overview

A Landmark of Dual Purpose

Rising from the redevelopment of Rajnigandha CHSL, this twin-identity development brings together I/O Tower and Residences within a single architectural statement in Oshiwara District Centre.

Sculpted with a striking facade, the tower is designed to be unmistakable on the Goregaon skyline — by day a study in rhythm and light, by dusk a glowing beacon.

  • Separate commercial & residential gates and lobbies
  • Basement parking (commercial) · podium parking (residential)
  • Terrace swimming pool, event lawn & party decks
  • Mini urban farming, fruit orchards & tree groove plaza
  • Passenger & fireman lifts with fire-check lobbies
  • BMS / fire control room & sewage treatment plant
